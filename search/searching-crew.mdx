CREW gives you two complementary ways to find exactly what your production needs: a universal search bar that spans every listing type at once, and four dedicated browse pages — Crew, Locations, Equipment, and Opportunities — each tuned with filters specific to that category. Whether you're hunting for a gaffer available next weekend or a soundstage in Austin, the tools below will get you there fast.

## Universal Search

The search bar at the top of every page on CREW searches across all listing types simultaneously. As you type, results appear grouped into four sections — **Crew**, **Locations**, **Equipment**, and **Opportunities** — so you can spot the right match at a glance without committing to a single category first.

Clicking any result in the dropdown takes you directly to that full listing page. If you press **Enter** or click the search icon, you'll land on a full results page with the same grouped layout and access to all filters.

<Note>
  The universal search bar is best for quick lookups by name or keyword. For
  power searches with multiple filters applied, head to the dedicated browse
  pages described below.
</Note>

### /crew

The crew browse page lists every crew member who has published a profile on CREW. You can filter by primary role, additional skills, city or travel radius, availability window, day rate, and union status. Results default to relevance ranking but can be sorted by newest profile, most reviewed, proximity, or lowest rate.

### /locations

The locations browse page covers indoor spaces, outdoor properties, working studios, soundstages, and practical environments available for production hire. Filters include location type, city or region, daily rate range, maximum crew capacity, available amenities such as parking and power access, and whether permits are required.

### /equipment

The equipment browse page lists gear available for daily or multi-day rental from owners across the country. Search by category — cameras, lighting, grip, audio, and more — or go straight to a brand or model number. Availability date filtering ensures you only see gear that's free on your shoot days.

### /opportunities

The opportunities browse page aggregates open calls posted by producers and production companies seeking crew. Each listing specifies the production type, role needed, pay structure, shoot window, and location so you can quickly assess fit before applying.

## Search Tips

Getting precise results is easier when you match the way CREW listings are written. Keep these tips in mind:

<Accordion title="Use role-specific industry terms">
  CREW listings use standard industry terminology. Search for **DP** rather
  than "camera person," **gaffer** rather than "lighting technician," or
  **script supervisor** rather than "continuity person" to surface the most
  relevant profiles.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Location search is flexible">
  The search bar and location filters both accept city names (e.g., `Los
  Angeles`), neighborhoods (e.g., `Silver Lake`), and ZIP codes (e.g.,
  `90026`). Use the radius slider on the crew and opportunities browse pages to
  expand results beyond an exact city match.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Equipment search supports brand names and model numbers">
  To find a specific camera body, type the brand and model directly into the
  search bar — for example, `ARRI ALEXA Mini LF` or `Sony FX9`. This works
  for lenses, lights, and audio gear too.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Use quotes for exact-match phrases">
  Wrapping a phrase in double quotes tells CREW to match those words in that
  exact order. For example, searching `"steadicam operator"` will return only
  listings that contain that exact phrase rather than results that mention
  "steadicam" and "operator" separately.

  ```text
  "steadicam operator"
  "ARRI ALEXA Mini LF"
  "practical location"
  ```
</Accordion>
