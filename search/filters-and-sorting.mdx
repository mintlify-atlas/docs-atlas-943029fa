With thousands of crew members, locations, equipment listings, and open opportunities on CREW, unfiltered search results can be overwhelming. Filters let you slice that pool down to only the listings that match your production's specific requirements — right location, right availability window, right budget — so you spend less time scrolling and more time making.

## Crew Filters

The following filters are available on the `/crew` browse page and on crew-scoped search results.

<Accordion title="Role">
  Filter by a crew member's **primary role** (e.g., Director of Photography,
  Sound Mixer, Production Designer) or by any of their **additional roles**. A
  single search can target one role or multiple roles at once.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Skills / Equipment Tags">
  Crew members tag their profiles with specific skills and equipment they're
  proficient with. Use this filter to find, for example, everyone who lists
  `DaVinci Resolve` under skills or `Steadicam` under equipment.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Location">
  Enter a **city name** or **ZIP code**, then use the **radius slider** to set
  how far from that point you want results — from 10 miles up to 500 miles (or
  the equivalent in kilometres).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Willing to Travel">
  Toggle to **Yes** to show only crew members who have indicated they're open
  to traveling for work. Toggle to **No** to show those who prefer local
  engagements only.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Availability Date Range">
  Set a **start date** and **end date** to surface only crew members who have
  marked themselves available for the full window you specify.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Day Rate Range">
  Use the **minimum** and **maximum** sliders to filter by the crew member's
  listed day rate in your local currency. Crew members who have not listed a
  rate are excluded when this filter is active.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Union Status">
  Choose **Union**, **Non-Union**, or **Either** to match your production's
  labor requirements.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Has Demo Reel">
  Toggle to **Yes** to show only crew members who have uploaded or linked a
  demo reel to their profile.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Reviews">
  Set a **minimum star rating** (1–5) to show only crew members whose average
  review score meets or exceeds that threshold.
</Accordion>

## Location Filters

The following filters are available on the `/locations` browse page.

<Accordion title="Location Type">
  Narrow by space category: **Indoor**, **Outdoor**, **Studio**, **Soundstage**,
  or **Practical**. You can select multiple types at once.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="City / Region">
  Search by city name or browse by region to limit results to a geographic area.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Daily Rate Range">
  Set minimum and maximum values to filter locations by their listed daily
  rental rate.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Capacity">
  Enter the maximum number of crew members your production will have on-site.
  Only locations that can accommodate at least that many people will appear.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Amenities">
  Check any combination of available amenities:
  - **Parking** — on-site or adjacent parking for crew vehicles
  - **Restrooms** — accessible bathroom facilities
  - **Kitchen** — catering-capable kitchen space
  - **Power Access** — production-grade power (e.g., 200A+)
  - **Wi-Fi** — on-site wireless internet
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Permits Required">
  Filter to locations where permits are **Required**, **Not Required**, or
  **Unknown** if you need certainty about the permitting process before
  reaching out.
</Accordion>

## Equipment Filters

The following filters are available on the `/equipment` browse page.

<Accordion title="Category">
  Browse by gear type: Cameras, Lenses, Lighting, Grip, Audio, Stabilization,
  Monitors, Power, and more.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Brand">
  Filter by manufacturer — for example, ARRI, Sony, RED, Cooke, Aputure, or
  Sennheiser — to narrow results to a specific brand family.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Availability Date Range">
  Set the dates your production needs the gear. Only items that are free for
  the entire window will appear.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Daily Rate Range">
  Set minimum and maximum day-rate values to match your equipment budget.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Condition">
  Filter by the owner's self-reported condition rating: **Excellent**, **Good**,
  or **Fair**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Pickup / Delivery Available">
  Toggle **Pickup Available** to show only gear you can collect in person, or
  **Delivery Available** to show items the owner will ship or courier to your
  set location.
</Accordion>

## Opportunity Filters

The following filters are available on the `/opportunities` browse page.

<Accordion title="Production Type">
  Filter open roles by the type of production: Feature Film, Short Film,
  Commercial, Music Video, Documentary, Web Series, and more.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Role Needed">
  Enter a specific crew role to find opportunities that are actively seeking
  that position.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Pay Type">
  Choose from **Paid**, **Deferred**, or **Unpaid** to match your compensation
  requirements.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Shoot Date Range">
  Set the earliest and latest acceptable shoot dates to surface only
  opportunities that fall within your available window.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Location">
  Filter by the city or region where the shoot will take place.
</Accordion>

## Sorting Options

After applying filters on the crew browse page, you can change the order in which results appear using the **Sort By** dropdown:

| Sort Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **Relevance** | Default. Ranks results by how closely the listing matches your search query and active filters. |
| **Newest Profile** | Shows the most recently created or updated profiles first — useful for finding freshly active crew. |
| **Most Reviewed** | Surfaces crew members with the highest number of reviews, regardless of rating. |
| **Closest to You** | Ranks results by geographic proximity to your detected or entered location. Requires location permission or a city filter to be active. |
| **Lowest Rate** | Orders results from the lowest listed day rate to the highest. Crew members without a listed rate appear at the end. |

<Tip>
  Found a filter combination you'll use again? Save it as a **Saved Search** to
  receive email alerts whenever new listings match your criteria. See
  [Saved Searches](/search/saved-searches) to learn how.
</Tip>
