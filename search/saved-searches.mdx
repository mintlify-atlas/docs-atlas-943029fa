Saved Searches let you bookmark any combination of search terms and filters so CREW can notify you the moment a new listing matches your criteria — without you having to check back manually. This feature is especially valuable for busy producers and filmmakers in active pre-production: instead of re-running the same searches every morning, you set your parameters once and let CREW surface the right people and places as they come available.

## Creating a Saved Search

Before saving, run a search and apply whatever filters reflect your real production needs. Once the results look right, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run a search with your filters applied">
    Navigate to any browse page — `/crew`, `/locations`, `/equipment`, or
    `/opportunities` — and apply the filters that define your ideal match. You
    can also start from the universal search bar and refine from the results
    page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Save Search button">
    At the top-right corner of the results panel, click the **Save Search**
    button. The button is visible whenever at least one search term or filter
    is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the search a descriptive name">
    In the dialog that appears, enter a name that will help you identify this
    search later — for example:

    ```text
    LA Cinematographers — Available March
    Austin Soundstages — Under $2k/day
    NYC Gaffers — Union Only
    ```

    Good names include the listing type, key filter values, and the production
    or timeframe they relate to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your alert frequency">
    Select how often you want CREW to email you when new matches appear:

    | Frequency | When you'll hear from us |
    |---|---|
    | **Instant** | Within one hour of a new matching listing being published (see limitations below) |
    | **Daily Digest** | Once per day, summarizing all new matches from the past 24 hours |
    | **Weekly Digest** | Once per week, summarizing all new matches from the past 7 days |
    | **None** | No emails — results are silently updated in your dashboard |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Click the **Save** button to confirm. The search is immediately added to
    your dashboard under **Saved Searches**, and alert emails will begin as
    soon as the first new match appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Saved Searches

All of your saved searches live in one place: the **Saved Searches** section of your CREW dashboard, accessible from your account menu.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="View All Saved Searches" icon="list" href="/search/saved-searches">
    See every saved search in a table with the search name, listing type, last
    run date, and the number of new results since you last viewed it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Edit Filters" icon="sliders" href="/search/filters-and-sorting">
    Click the **Edit** button on any saved search to reopen the filter panel
    and adjust your criteria without losing the search name or alert settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Change Alert Frequency" icon="bell" href="/profiles/account-settings">
    Switch between Instant, Daily Digest, Weekly Digest, and None at any time
    from the saved search's settings menu.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Delete a Saved Search" icon="trash" href="/search/saved-searches">
    Click **Delete** on any saved search to remove it permanently. This also
    cancels any associated alert emails immediately.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The **New Results** count next to each saved search shows how many listings have been added since you last clicked through to view that search's results. This counter resets each time you open the results.

## Alert Emails

When CREW sends you a saved search alert, the email is formatted to give you a quick preview without requiring you to log in to evaluate every match.

Each alert email includes:

- **Subject line** — formatted as `[Saved Search] {Search Name} — {N} new match(es)`, for example: `[Saved Search] LA Cinematographers — Available March — 3 new matches`
- **Up to 5 new listing previews** — each preview shows the listing title, primary category tag, location, rate (where listed), and a one-sentence description pulled from the listing itself
- **View All Matches button** — a single click takes you directly to the full filtered results page inside CREW, where all new matches are highlighted

<Note>
  Alert emails are sent to the address associated with your CREW account. To
  update your email address, visit your account settings.
</Note>

## Limitations

Keep the following constraints in mind when planning your saved search strategy:

<Warning>
  **Maximum 20 saved searches per account.** If you reach the limit, you must
  delete an existing saved search before creating a new one. Consider
  consolidating searches that cover similar criteria.
</Warning>

- **Saved searches expire after 12 months of inactivity.** A search is considered inactive if no one has viewed its results and no alert emails have been opened during that period. CREW will send a reminder email 30 days before expiry so you can re-activate it.
- **Instant alerts are sent at most once per hour per search.** If multiple new matching listings are published within the same hour, they are batched into a single email rather than triggering individual notifications.

<Tip>
  Create a separate saved search for each production you're currently in
  pre-production on — for example, one for crew, one for locations, and one for
  equipment on each project. Keeping them separate means your dashboard and
  alert emails stay organized by production rather than mixing results across
  projects.
</Tip>
