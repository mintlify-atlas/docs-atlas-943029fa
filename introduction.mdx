CREW is the all-in-one platform built for the film industry. Whether you're a director assembling your next short film, a producer staffing a feature, a gaffer looking for your next gig, or a location manager sourcing the perfect backdrop, CREW connects every moving part of a production in one place. From seasoned cinematographers to emerging grips, from urban rooftops to rural farmsteads, CREW brings together the people, places, and gear that make films happen.

## What you can do with CREW

CREW is organized around four core capabilities that cover the full lifecycle of staffing and resourcing a production.

- **Find Crew** — Search a growing network of professionals by role, skill set, location, and availability. Filter by credits, day rate, and union status to find exactly who you need.
- **Scout Locations** — Browse a curated library of film-ready locations complete with photos, permit information, and direct contact with location owners.
- **Rent Equipment** — Access the equipment marketplace to find cameras, lenses, lighting, audio gear, and grip packages available for rent near your shoot.
- **Post & Find Opportunities** — Filmmakers can post paid and unpaid production opportunities; crew members can browse and apply to roles that match their skills and schedule.
