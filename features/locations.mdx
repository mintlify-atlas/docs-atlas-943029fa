Finding the perfect location is one of the most time-consuming parts of pre-production, and CREW is built to make it faster. The platform hosts thousands of verified filming locations submitted by property owners — from raw industrial warehouses and rooftop terraces to dense forests, working restaurants, and purpose-built studios. Every listing includes photos, an availability calendar, and a direct line to the owner, so you can scout, shortlist, and reach out without leaving the platform.

## Browsing Locations

Use the search bar and filter panel on the Locations page to find spaces that match your production's needs. Filters can be combined freely to narrow your results.

Available filters include:

- **Location type** — Indoor, Outdoor, Studio, or Practical (real-world functional spaces)
- **City or region** — search by city name or draw a radius on the map view
- **Size** — square footage or capacity brackets
- **Amenities** — power access, restrooms, changing rooms, kitchen, parking, loading dock, and more
- **Daily rate** — set a minimum and maximum to stay on budget
- **Availability** — select your shoot dates to surface only locations that are open

## Location Listing Details

Each location listing is designed to give scouts and producers everything they need to make a decision before reaching out to an owner.

A listing includes:

- **Photos** — up to 20 high-resolution images across different angles and times of day
- **Address** — shown as an approximate neighborhood until a booking is confirmed, at which point the full address is released
- **Amenities checklist** — a standardized list covering power, restrooms, Wi-Fi, kitchen, parking, and more
- **Availability calendar** — a live calendar showing blocked and open dates
- **Daily and half-day rate** — clearly listed in the local currency
- **Parking details** — number of spaces, street parking notes, or loading zone access
- **Permits required** — a yes/no flag indicating whether the owner is aware of permit requirements for filming
- **Max crew size** — the maximum number of people the space can accommodate during a shoot

## Listing Your Location

If you own or manage a space that would work for film and video productions, listing it on CREW is straightforward. All new listings are reviewed by CREW moderators before going live.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Add Listing">
    From your dashboard, click **Add Listing** and select **Location** from the listing type menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add title, description, and photos">
    Write a clear title and a detailed description of the space — dimensions, natural light, noise levels, and any quirks scouts should know about. Upload up to 20 photos. Better photos lead to more inquiries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set availability and pricing">
    Use the availability calendar to block out dates the space is unavailable. Set your daily rate and, optionally, a half-day rate and security deposit amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Click **Submit for Review**. CREW moderators review all new location listings within 24 hours. You'll receive an email notification when your listing is approved and live on the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never share the exact address of your property in public-facing listing fields, messages before booking, or external communications. CREW reveals full address details automatically to both parties only after a booking is confirmed. Sharing addresses early bypasses this protection and voids CREW's booking policies.
</Warning>
