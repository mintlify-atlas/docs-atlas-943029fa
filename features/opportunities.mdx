The CREW opportunity board is where productions find people and people find productions. Filmmakers, producers, and production companies use it to advertise open roles on upcoming projects — everything from fully-paid feature films to student shorts seeking experienced crew who want the credit. Crew members on the other side browse the board, filter for opportunities that match their skills and availability, and apply directly through the platform. Whether you're building a roster for a big commercial or looking for your next creative project, the opportunity board is the fastest way to make that connection.

## For Filmmakers — Posting an Opportunity

Posting an opportunity takes only a few minutes. Your listing goes live immediately and is visible to all CREW members as soon as you publish it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Dashboard → Post Opportunity">
    From your filmmaker dashboard, click **Post Opportunity** in the top navigation bar or the quick-action panel on the dashboard home screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the opportunity details">
    Complete the listing form with the following information:

    - **Project title** — the name of the production or a working title
    - **Production type** — Film, TV, Commercial, Music Video, Student, or Other
    - **Role(s) needed** — add one or more roles you're hiring for; each role can have its own pay type and description
    - **Pay type** — Paid, Deferred, Unpaid, or TBD
    - **Shoot dates** — exact dates or an estimated range
    - **Location** — city and region where principal photography takes place
    - **Description** — a full overview of the project, the vibe, the story, what you're looking for, and any relevant context that would help a crew member decide to apply
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally attach your filmmaker profile">
    Linking your CREW filmmaker profile to the opportunity gives applicants instant context — your previous credits, your reel, and your reputation on the platform. It's optional but strongly recommended.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**. The listing goes live immediately on the opportunity board and is discoverable through search and browse. You'll receive a notification in your dashboard each time someone applies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## For Crew — Finding Opportunities

The opportunity board is accessible from the main navigation under **Opportunities**. By default, listings are sorted by most recently posted, but you can sort by shoot date or relevance based on your profile's listed skills and roles.

Use the filter panel to narrow the board to opportunities that fit your situation:

- **Production type** — filter to the genres or formats you work in
- **Role** — surface only listings seeking your specific position
- **Pay type** — show only paid work, or include deferred and student projects
- **Location** — filter by city, region, or remote-friendly opportunities
- **Shoot date** — enter your available window to hide listings that conflict with your schedule

When you find an opportunity you want to pursue, click **Apply**. A message compose window will open where you can write a short note introducing yourself and explaining why you're a good fit. Your CREW profile is automatically attached to the application — make sure it's up to date with your latest credits and reel before applying.

## Opportunity Status

Every listing moves through a clear lifecycle so both filmmakers and crew members always know where things stand.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | The listing is live and accepting applications. |
| **In Review** | The filmmaker is actively reviewing applicants and may have paused new applications temporarily. |
| **Filled** | The role has been cast and no further applications are being accepted. |
| **Closed** | The listing has been taken down by the filmmaker or expired. |

Filmmakers can mark individual roles within a multi-role listing as **Filled** without closing the entire opportunity — so if you've found your DP but are still searching for a sound mixer, only the sound mixer role stays open and visible to applicants.

<Warning>
  CREW does not verify the pay or compensation claims made in opportunity listings. It is your responsibility to confirm all payment details, rates, deferral terms, and any other compensation arrangements in writing — ideally within the CREW message thread — before committing to a project. Never rely on the listing alone as a binding agreement.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Once you've posted an opportunity, keep all your open listings organized and up to date from one place. See [Managing Project Listings](/collaboration/project-listings) for how to edit roles, update status, and review applicants across multiple productions at once.
</Tip>
