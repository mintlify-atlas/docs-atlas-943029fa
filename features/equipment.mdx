Owning every piece of gear a production needs is rarely practical, and rental houses aren't always the most flexible or affordable option. CREW's equipment marketplace connects filmmakers directly with individual owners who rent out their cameras, lenses, lighting rigs, audio packages, drones, and more. Listings come from verified owners across the country, and each one includes condition notes, included accessories, daily and weekly rates, and deposit requirements — so you know exactly what you're getting before you send a single message.

## Searching for Equipment

From the Equipment page, use the search bar to type a specific brand or item name — "Sony FX6", "Aputure 600d", or just "anamorphic lenses" — and CREW will surface matching listings. Combine the search with filters to refine results further.

Available filters include:

- **Category** — select one or more of the categories listed above
- **Brand or model** — narrow results to a specific manufacturer or product line
- **Location** — city, region, or distance from your shoot location
- **Daily rate** — set a ceiling to stay within your equipment budget
- **Availability dates** — enter your shoot window to see only gear that's free when you need it

## Equipment Listing Details

Every equipment listing includes the information renters need to evaluate an item before reaching out.

A listing includes:

- **Item name** — clear, searchable title including brand and model
- **Brand and model** — the manufacturer and exact product designation
- **Condition** — rated as Excellent, Good, or Fair, with the owner's notes on any wear
- **Included accessories** — a list of everything that ships with the item (batteries, cases, cables, adapters)
- **Daily rate** — the base per-day rental price
- **Weekly rate** — a discounted rate for rentals of five or more days
- **Deposit amount** — a security deposit the renter agrees to before confirming the rental
- **Pickup and delivery options** — whether the item is pickup-only, available for local delivery, or can be shipped
- **Owner location** — the city and neighborhood where the gear is based

## Listing Your Equipment

If you own gear that sits idle between your own shoots, listing it on CREW is a straightforward way to offset ownership costs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Add Listing">
    From your dashboard, click **Add Listing** and select **Equipment** from the listing type menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter item details and upload photos">
    Fill in the item name, brand, model, condition, and a description of included accessories. Upload clear photos — at least one showing the item, one showing any wear, and one showing what's included in the case or bag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set rates and availability">
    Enter your daily rate, optional weekly rate, and deposit amount. Use the availability calendar to block out dates when the gear is in use or unavailable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your listing">
    Click **Publish**. Your listing goes live immediately and renters can find it through search, browse it by category, and message you to arrange a rental.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Add serial numbers and high-resolution photos of any pre-existing scratches, dents, or wear marks to your listing before you rent to anyone. This creates a clear, timestamped record that protects you from disputes if a renter claims damage that was already there.
</Tip>

<Note>
  CREW does not process payments for equipment rentals. Use the built-in messaging system to communicate with the owner and arrange payment, deposit, and pickup details directly. Always agree on terms in writing within the CREW message thread before exchanging gear.
</Note>
