Finding the right people for your production can make or break a project. CREW's discovery engine gives filmmakers and producers a single place to search thousands of verified film professionals — from seasoned directors of photography to production assistants just starting out — and reach out to them directly. Whether you're staffing a feature film, a commercial shoot, or a weekend short, the crew discovery tools help you move from empty call sheet to fully crewed production as quickly as possible.

## Searching for Crew

The search experience is built around speed and precision. Type a role, a name, or a skill into the search bar and CREW returns a ranked list of matching profiles. From there, filters let you narrow the results until you're looking at exactly the right candidates for your production.

Available filters include:

- **Role** — Director, DP, Gaffer, Editor, and dozens more
- **Location** — city, region, or a radius around a specific point
- **Skills** — specific software, techniques, or equipment proficiencies
- **Availability** — a date range to find crew who are free when you need them
- **Union status** — union, non-union, or both
- **Rate range** — daily or weekly rate brackets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter a role or name">
    Type into the search bar at the top of the Crew page — try a job title like "Gaffer" or a person's name if you already have someone in mind.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Use the filter panel on the left to narrow results by location, availability dates, union status, and rate range. Filters apply instantly without reloading the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review profiles">
    Click any result to open the full crew profile — view their reel, credits, skills, gear, and reviews from past collaborators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a message or save to project">
    Hit **Message** to start a conversation directly, or click **Save to Project** to add them to a project shortlist for later follow-up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saving Crew to a Project

When you find someone promising but aren't ready to reach out yet, save them to a project so you can compare candidates and come back to them later.

On any crew profile, click the **Save to Project** button and select an existing project from the dropdown — or create a new one on the spot. Saved crew members appear in your project's **Crew** tab, where you can add notes, track your outreach status, and build your shortlist before sending any messages.

<Tip>
  Reaching out with a detailed message about your production — shoot dates, location, the tone of the project, and why you thought of this person specifically — gets significantly better response rates than a generic "Hi". Crew members are more likely to reply when they can see you've read their profile.
</Tip>
