CREW's built-in messaging system lets you reach out to crew members, location owners, and equipment providers without ever leaving the platform. Keeping conversations on CREW means both parties have a shared, searchable record of every exchange, listing details stay attached to the thread for easy reference, and CREW's trust and safety tools remain available throughout the collaboration process.

## Starting a Conversation

There are two ways to kick off a new message thread on CREW:

1. **From a listing page** — Navigate to any crew profile, location listing, or equipment listing and click the **Message [Name]** button. This automatically opens a new thread pre-linked to that listing.
2. **From the Inbox** — If you've previously connected with someone, open your Inbox and click **New Message**, then search for the person by name or CREW username to resume or start a separate thread.

Once a thread is open, the conversation view displays messages in chronological order with timestamps. The composer sits at the bottom of the screen — type your message and press **Send** or hit `Enter`.

## Conversation Context

When a thread is started from a listing page, a listing card is pinned to the top of the conversation for the lifetime of the thread. The card shows the listing title, thumbnail, and category so both parties always have an at-a-glance reminder of the specific role, location, or piece of equipment being discussed — especially useful when you're managing multiple threads simultaneously.

Threads started directly from the Inbox (without a listing) do not display a pinned card, though you can manually paste a listing URL into the conversation at any time.

## Message Features

CREW's messaging system includes several features to keep your conversations clear and efficient:

- **Plain text messages** — all messages are plain text; markdown formatting is not rendered in the composer or thread view.
- **Photo and file attachments** — attach files up to **10 MB** each; supported formats are JPG, PNG, and PDF.
- **Read receipts** — a small checkmark icon appears beneath your message once the recipient has opened it.
- **Reply to a specific message** — hover over any message and click the reply arrow to quote it inline, keeping long threads easy to follow.
- **Archive a conversation** — move a thread out of your main Inbox once it's no longer active without permanently deleting it.
- **Report a message** — flag a message that violates CREW's community guidelines; the report is sent directly to the trust and safety team.

## Notification Settings

By default, CREW sends an email and an in-app push notification for every new message. You can adjust notification frequency — including daily digest mode or turning off email notifications entirely — in your account settings.

<Warning>
  Never share personal payment information — including bank account details, card numbers, or wire transfer instructions — in CREW messages. CREW does not process payments and cannot recover funds lost to fraud. Always use a trusted third-party payment method negotiated outside the platform.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  When reaching out to a crew member for the first time, attach your call sheet or relevant script pages as a PDF. Giving them concrete context about the project upfront helps them assess whether it's a good fit — and makes your inquiry stand out.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Messages sent from accounts that have no profile photo and no bio are automatically routed to the recipient's **Filtered** folder rather than their main Inbox. Complete your profile to ensure your messages are seen promptly.
</Note>
