A **Project** in CREW is a virtual production board scoped to a single film or production. Instead of juggling bookmarks, spreadsheets, and scattered notes, a Project brings your shortlisted crew profiles, location listings, equipment, and opportunity posts under one roof — giving you and your collaborators a single source of truth from pre-production through to your shoot days.

## Creating a Project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Projects section">
    From your Dashboard, navigate to **Projects** in the left sidebar, then click **New Project**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter project details">
    Fill in the **project title**, select a **production type** (e.g. Feature, Short, Commercial, Music Video, Documentary), and set your **estimated shoot dates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an optional logline">
    Write a short logline or production description in the optional description field. This text is shown to crew members when you invite them to the project, giving them instant context about what they're potentially joining.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your project">
    Click **Create Project**. Your new project board opens immediately and is ready for you to start adding resources.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding Resources to a Project

Projects support four resource types, each managed on its own dedicated tab within the project board.

**Crew**

Browse to any crew member's profile and click **Save to Project**, then select the relevant project from the dropdown. Once saved, you can assign the crew member a **role** (e.g. Director of Photography, Gaffer, Production Designer) and set a **status**:

- **Contacted** — you've reached out but haven't heard back
- **Interested** — they've expressed interest
- **Confirmed** — they're locked in
- **Passed** — not available or not the right fit

**Locations**

Open any location listing and click **Save to Project** to pin it to your board. Saved locations appear in the Locations tab alongside their listing thumbnail, availability, and your internal status.

**Equipment**

Equipment listings can be saved the same way — visit a listing and click **Save to Project**. Track multiple equipment items across vendors in one place.

**Opportunities**

Opportunity listings you've posted (e.g. open casting calls or crew calls) can be linked to a project from the Opportunities tab. This lets you manage incoming applicants in the context of the production they're applying to.

## Project Status Columns

Each resource tab displays a **kanban-style board** with four columns you can drag cards between:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Shortlisted** | Added to the project but no action taken yet |
| **Contacted** | Outreach has been made |
| **Confirmed** | Committed to the production |
| **Passed** | No longer being considered |

Moving a card between columns updates its status in real time and is visible to anyone you've shared the project with.

## Sharing Projects

Projects are **private by default** — only you can see them. To bring in collaborators such as a co-producer or assistant director, click **Share Project** in the top-right corner of the project board and enter their CREW username. Invited collaborators receive **view access**, meaning they can see all saved resources and statuses but cannot add, remove, or reorder cards.

<Tip>
  Use the **Notes** field on each saved crew card to log key details from your conversations — availability windows, rate discussions, or anything else relevant. Co-producers and ADs with shared access can read these notes in real time, keeping the whole team aligned without extra back-and-forth.
</Tip>
