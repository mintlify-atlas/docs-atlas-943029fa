Reputation is the currency of the film industry. Whether you're a gaffer looking for your next feature or a producer assembling a crew from scratch, a track record of professional, high-quality work is the fastest way to open doors. CREW's verified review system formalizes that reputation in one place — turning word-of-mouth into a transparent, durable public record that travels with your profile.

## How Reviews Work

CREW's review system is built around verified, post-collaboration feedback:

- **Reviews are triggered after a collaboration is marked complete.** Either party can mark a project collaboration complete, which sends a review request notification to both parties.
- **Both parties receive a notification** prompting them to leave a review for the other person.
- **A review consists of a star rating (1–5) and a written review** of a minimum of 30 characters and a maximum of 500 characters.
- **Reviews are published after both parties have submitted**, or automatically after **14 days** if only one party has submitted theirs.
- **CREW verifies the connection** before allowing a review to be submitted — both parties must have been connected via the platform as part of the collaboration in question. This prevents fraudulent or unverified reviews.

## Leaving a Review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Go to your **Inbox** and find the conversation thread with the person you'd like to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Leave a Review'">
    Once the associated project has been marked complete, a **Leave a Review** button appears at the top of the conversation thread. Click it to open the review form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a star rating">
    Choose an overall star rating between **1** (poor) and **5** (excellent).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your review">
    Write your review in the text field. Be specific and professional — your review is public and reflects on you as well as the person you're reviewing. Minimum 30 characters, maximum 500 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit Review**. Your review is held in escrow until the other party submits theirs, or until 14 days have passed — whichever comes first. After that, both reviews (if submitted) are published simultaneously on each party's profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In addition to the overall **1–5 star rating**, every review includes scores across three sub-dimensions. These sub-scores are displayed individually on the reviewed person's profile, giving visitors a granular view of their strengths:

## Responding to a Review

You can write a **one-time public response** to any review posted on your profile. To respond, navigate to the review on your profile page and click **Respond**. Your response is displayed directly below the review, visible to anyone who views your profile.

Responses are a professional tool — use them to provide context, acknowledge feedback graciously, or address any factual inaccuracies. Responses cannot be edited after submission, so take care to keep your tone constructive.

## Dispute Policy

If you believe a review posted on your profile is fraudulent, retaliatory, or violates CREW's community guidelines, you can flag it for review by the trust team.

<Accordion title="How to report a review">
  Navigate to the review on your profile, click the **three-dot menu** in the top-right corner of the review card, and select **Report Review**. In the form that appears, select the reason for your report and add any supporting context. CREW's trust team will review your report within **5 business days** and notify you of the outcome via email.
</Accordion>

<Warning>
  Reviews cannot be edited after submission. Before you click Submit, re-read your review carefully to ensure it is accurate, fair, and professional. Inaccurate or malicious reviews may be removed by CREW's trust team, which can affect your own standing on the platform.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Ask your collaborator to leave a review immediately after the shoot wraps — while the experience is still fresh. Response rates drop significantly as time passes. A quick message in your CREW thread right after the final day on set is all it takes to prompt a timely, detailed review.
</Tip>
