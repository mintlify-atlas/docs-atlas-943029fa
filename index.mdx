## How CREW Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your profile">
    Sign up as a filmmaker or crew member. Add your skills, credits, portfolio links, and availability so the right people can find you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or post">
    Use CREW's powerful search to find crew, locations, and equipment — or post a listing to attract talent and resources to your production.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and message">
    Send direct messages to crew members, location owners, and equipment providers. Discuss details, negotiate terms, and confirm bookings — all inside CREW.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collaborate and review">
    Complete your production and leave reviews for everyone involved. Build your reputation and help the community grow.
  </Step>
</Steps>
