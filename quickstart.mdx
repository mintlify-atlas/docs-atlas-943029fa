This guide walks you through everything you need to go from zero to your first connection on CREW. By the end you'll have a live profile, know how to search for crew, locations, and equipment, and be ready to send your first message or apply to your first opportunity — all in about five minutes.

<Note>
  Profiles with a completed photo and bio receive **3× more responses** than incomplete profiles. Take a few extra minutes on Step 3 — it pays off.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Navigate to [crew.app](https://crew.app) and click **Sign Up** in the top-right corner. Enter your full name, email address, and a secure password, then click **Create Account**. You'll receive a verification email — click the link inside to activate your account before continuing.

    <Tip>
      Use a professional email address (e.g., your production company domain) rather than a personal one. It builds credibility with other users from the moment they see your profile.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your role">
    After verifying your email you'll be prompted to choose a primary role:

    - **Filmmaker / Producer** — you're here to staff productions, find locations, source gear, and post opportunities.
    - **Crew Member** — you're here to get discovered, showcase your work, and find your next gig.

    Pick the role that best describes your primary use case. Don't worry about locking yourself in — you can add a second role at any time from **Account Settings → Roles**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    A complete profile is your calling card on CREW. Fill in the following fields to maximize your visibility:

    - **Profile photo** — a clear, professional headshot or production still.
    - **Bio** — a short paragraph (2–4 sentences) describing your experience and what you're looking for.
    - **Location** — your primary city and country so nearby productions can find you.
    - **Skills** *(Crew Members)* — add your key skills and department (e.g., Camera, Lighting, Sound).
    - **Project types** *(Filmmakers)* — indicate the kinds of projects you typically produce (feature, short, commercial, documentary, etc.).
    - **Portfolio link** — add a link to your IMDb page, personal website, or reel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make your first search">
    Use the **Search** bar at the top of any page to start exploring. Here are a few ways to get going:

    - **Find crew by role and location** — try searching `"cinematographer Los Angeles"` or `"sound mixer New York"`.
    - **Browse locations** — click **Locations** in the main navigation and filter by city, property type, or permit status.
    - **Check the equipment marketplace** — click **Equipment** to browse cameras, lenses, lighting, and grip packages available near you.

    Use the filter panel on the left side of any results page to narrow by availability, day rate, skills, or listing type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect">
    Once you've found someone or something interesting, take action:

    - **Message a crew member** — open their profile and click **Send Message** to start a conversation tied to a specific project context.
    - **Save a location** — click the bookmark icon on any location listing to save it to one of your Projects for easy reference later.
    - **Apply to an opportunity** — open an Opportunity listing and click **Apply Now** to submit your profile directly to the posting filmmaker.
  </Step>
</Steps>
